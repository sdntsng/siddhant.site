# Voice Guide

## Shared voice

Siddhant should sound like a technically curious founder who understands marketing, creative work, systems, and commercial reality.

The voice is:

- direct, thoughtful, and specific;
- ambitious without inflated claims;
- technically informed without performing expertise;
- personal when lived experience improves the argument;
- willing to state uncertainty and limits;
- more interested in mechanisms than buzzwords.

## Avoid

- generic opening hooks such as “AI is changing everything”;
- motivational filler;
- manufactured certainty;
- excessive rhetorical questions;
- false binaries;
- unexplained jargon;
- calling every automation an agent;
- breathless summaries of product launches;
- self-congratulatory founder storytelling;
- identical copy pasted across channels.

## LinkedIn

**Role:** the primary professional thought-leadership channel.

Write in compact paragraphs. Lead with an observation or claim rather than a theatrical hook. Use operating examples, frameworks, and consequences for decision-makers. A post should usually make one argument well.

Typical length: 300–900 words.

Best endings:
- a clear implication;
- a practical test;
- a genuine question that advances the discussion.

## Blog

**Role:** canonical, durable body of work.

The blog should carry the complete argument, evidence, diagrams, caveats, and links. It can be more technical and reflective than social content. Search value matters, but the prose should not read as SEO copy.

Typical length: 1,200–3,000 words.

Use the repository’s MDX standards and approved tags.

## X

**Role:** idea testing, compressed observations, and participation in current technical discourse.

Prefer sharp claims and concrete mechanisms. Threads should earn their length. Individual posts can be more informal and experimental than LinkedIn, but not glib.

Useful formats:
- one-line claim plus example;
- 4–10 post argument;
- response to a paper, launch, chart, or practitioner debate;
- build note with a specific finding.

## Instagram

**Role:** visual education and founder familiarity.

Carousels should simplify without flattening. Each slide should carry one useful idea. Reels should sound spoken, not like an essay read aloud.

Useful formats:
- 6–8 slide framework;
- founder-to-camera explanation;
- annotated workflow or system diagram;
- before/after process comparison;
- visual case study.

## Voice calibration examples

Too generic:
> AI will revolutionize marketing by enabling personalization at scale.

Better:
> Personalization is not the difficult part anymore. The difficult part is deciding which variation deserves to exist, proving it is on-brand, and learning from thousands of outputs without drowning the team in review.

Too promotional:
> At Vinci, we are redefining the future of creativity.

Better:
> Building generative creative systems has made one constraint obvious: lower generation cost does not remove creative judgment. It moves judgment into briefs, evaluation, selection, and feedback loops.
