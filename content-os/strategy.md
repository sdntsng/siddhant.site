# Content Strategy

## North star

Build Siddhant into a credible, distinctive voice at the intersection of **agentic marketing, AI-native creative production, and operational AI systems**.

The content should create three outcomes:

1. **Authority:** establish original thinking that practitioners reference.
2. **Demand:** attract clients, collaborators, talent, and investors relevant to Vinci and Siddhant's AI work.
3. **Compounding intellectual property:** turn operating lessons into a searchable body of work on `siddhant.site`.

## Editorial pillars

### 1. Agentic marketing

How marketing changes when software can research, reason, generate, test, deploy, learn, and coordinate.

Key questions:
- What should remain human-led?
- Where do agents create real leverage rather than theatre?
- How should brands reorganize teams, workflows, and governance?

### 2. AI-native creative systems

The production and operating systems behind generative media: creative velocity, evaluation, consistency, adaptation, model choice, economics, and quality control.

### 3. Memory and context for agents

Why durable, governed memory is essential for useful agents; the distinction between chat history and operational truth; provenance, recency, and memory safety.

### 4. Building AI companies

Founder notes from building Vinci and other AI systems: distribution, services versus software, technical strategy, enterprise adoption, margins, hiring, and speed.

### 5. India and emerging-market AI

How AI adoption, creative production, language, distribution, and entrepreneurship differ outside the default Silicon Valley frame.

## Content mix

- **40% original frameworks and arguments**
- **25% operating lessons and case studies**
- **15% technical explainers for business readers**
- **10% timely analysis of important developments**
- **10% personal founder reflections**

## Default cadence

- LinkedIn: 3 substantive posts per week
- X: 4–7 posts per week, including one thread
- Instagram: 2 carousels or reels per week
- Blog: 2 deep pieces per month

Cadence is subordinate to quality. One important argument should generate multiple channel-native assets without becoming repetitive.

## Editorial tests

Before drafting, ask:

1. Is the claim specific enough to disagree with?
2. Does Siddhant have earned insight or evidence here?
3. Is this useful to a founder, marketer, or AI operator?
4. Can the idea survive beyond this week's news cycle?
5. What is the strongest counterargument?
6. Is there a concrete example, model, diagram, or number that makes it memorable?
