# Analytics Framework

## Measurement principle

The objective is not maximum reach. The objective is to build authority with the right audience and create measurable downstream value.

## Core metrics

### Attention

- impressions or reach;
- profile visits;
- video watch time and completion;
- blog page views and engaged time.

### Depth

- saves or bookmarks;
- shares or reposts;
- comments with substantive responses;
- thread completion or carousel completion where available;
- blog scroll depth and return visits.

### Conversion

- qualified inbound messages;
- newsletter or contact conversions;
- client or partner conversations influenced;
- speaking, podcast, press, or collaboration requests;
- candidate or talent interest;
- backlinks and citations.

## Useful derived metrics

- **Engagement rate:** engagements / impressions
- **Save rate:** saves / impressions
- **Share rate:** shares / impressions
- **Conversation rate:** substantive comments + qualified replies / impressions
- **Inbound rate:** qualified inbound / profile visits
- **Blog conversion rate:** desired actions / unique page views

Use derived metrics directionally because platform definitions differ.

## Review windows

Record performance at:

- 24 hours: distribution and opening strength;
- 7 days: durable engagement and conversations;
- 30 days: search, long-tail distribution, inbound, and citations.

## Monthly review questions

1. Which ideas attracted the audience Siddhant wants, rather than merely the largest audience?
2. Which pieces generated saves, thoughtful disagreement, or qualified inbound?
3. Which pillars consistently underperform, and is the issue topic, execution, or audience fit?
4. Which social posts should become durable blog essays?
5. Which blog essays should become updated visual assets?
6. What questions appear repeatedly in comments and private messages?
7. Which claims need more evidence or a revised position?

## Scoring model

Each published asset can receive a 1–5 score for:

- audience quality;
- idea originality;
- engagement depth;
- commercial relevance;
- durability;
- ease of repurposing.

The total score is more useful for editorial decisions than ranking by impressions alone.
