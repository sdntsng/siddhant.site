# Siddhant Content OS

This directory is the operating system for Siddhant Singh's public writing across:

- `siddhant.site`
- LinkedIn
- X
- Instagram

The goal is not to publish four copies of the same post. The goal is to develop one strong, researched thesis and express it natively for each channel.

## Positioning

**Core territory:** agentic marketing, AI-native creative systems, generative media, operational memory, and the realities of building AI companies.

**Audience:** founders, marketing leaders, agency leaders, product and AI operators, investors, and ambitious builders.

**Promise:** practical, technically informed writing from someone building and deploying these systems—not generic AI commentary.

## Weekly production model

1. Select one thesis from the editorial calendar.
2. Produce a research brief with primary sources, counterarguments, and examples.
3. Draft the canonical long-form argument.
4. Adapt it for each channel:
   - Blog: rigorous, durable, searchable.
   - LinkedIn: founder-led argument with a clear business implication.
   - X: compressed claims, observations, and threads.
   - Instagram: visual explanation, carousel, or founder-led reel script.
5. Publish, capture analytics, and log qualitative responses.
6. Revisit strong ideas after 30–90 days with a sharper angle or new evidence.

## Source of truth

The Google Sheet **Siddhant Content OS** is the operational tracker for calendar, research, drafts, publishing status, and analytics.

The repository is the source of truth for written material, research notes, and publishable blog files.

## Editorial standard

Every substantial piece should contain:

- one defensible central claim;
- evidence or direct operating experience;
- an implication for the reader;
- at least one tension, limitation, or counterargument;
- a point of view that Siddhant is willing to own publicly.

Avoid trend summaries that could have been written by anyone. Prefer arguments that combine technical understanding, business judgment, and lived operating experience.
